# Defensive Ratings in Diamond Mind Baseball - Simple Breakdown

## Rating Format

**Example:** `Ex/56` or `Vg/33` or `Fr/223`

This means: **Range Rating / Error Rating**

---

## Range Rating (First Part)

**Scale:** Ex > Vg > Av > Fr > Pr

- **Ex** = Excellent range (best)
- **Vg** = Very Good range
- **Av** = Average range
- **Fr** = Fair range
- **Pr** = Poor range (worst)

**What it means:** How many balls the player gets to (range/coverage).

**Example:**
- `Ex/56` = Excellent range, 56 error rating
- `Vg/33` = Very Good range, 33 error rating
- `Fr/91` = Fair range, 91 error rating

---

## Error Rating (Second Part - The Number)

**The number represents a percentage of league average errors.**

**Key rule:** **LOWER IS BETTER**

### Examples:
- **33 ERR** = Makes 33% of league average errors (makes FEWER errors - excellent!)
- **56 ERR** = Makes 56% of league average errors (makes fewer errors - good!)
- **82 ERR** = Makes 82% of league average errors (makes slightly fewer - decent)
- **100 ERR** = Makes exactly league average errors
- **142 ERR** = Makes 142% of league average errors (makes MORE errors - bad!)
- **223 ERR** = Makes 223% of league average errors (makes WAY MORE errors - terrible!)

---

## Simple Translation

**Error Rating = Percentage of Average Errors**

| Rating | Meaning | Quality |
|--------|---------|---------|
| 0-50 | Makes 0-50% of average errors | Excellent |
| 51-75 | Makes 51-75% of average errors | Very Good |
| 76-100 | Makes 76-100% of average errors | Good to Average |
| 101-125 | Makes 101-125% of average errors | Below Average |
| 126-150 | Makes 126-150% of average errors | Poor |
| 151+ | Makes 151%+ of average errors | Terrible |

---

## What Matters More: Range or Errors?

**Both matter, but:**
- **Range** affects how many balls they get to (hits prevented)
- **Errors** affect how many mistakes they make (runs allowed)

**Rule of thumb:**
- Each range grade = ~10 hits saved per season
- Each error costs ~0.5-1.1 runs (varies by position)

**For defense-first teams:** You want **low error ratings** (especially at critical positions like SS, CF, C).

---

## Position-Specific Error Costs

**Cost per error varies by position:**

| Position | Cost per Error |
|----------|----------------|
| CF | 1.13 runs (highest - most costly!) |
| LF | 0.88 runs |
| C | 0.79 runs |
| 1B | 0.79 runs |
| RF | 0.63 runs |
| SS | 0.62 runs |
| 3B | 0.54 runs |
| 2B | 0.50 runs (lowest cost) |

**Key insight:** Errors at CF cost more than errors at 2B, so prioritize low errors at CF.

---

## Quick Reference

**When comparing players:**
1. **Range:** Higher grade is better (Ex > Vg > Av > Fr > Pr)
2. **Error Rating:** Lower number is better (33 < 56 < 82 < 142 < 223)

**Best defensive players have:**
- High range rating (Ex or Vg)
- Low error rating (0-60)

**Your goal:** Minimize error ratings, especially at SS, CF, C, and outfield positions.
